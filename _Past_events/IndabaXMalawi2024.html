SUMMARY

This dynamic event took place in the vibrant city of Blantyre, Malawi on August 23 – 24, 2024. The two-day gathering hoped to bring together a diverse group of professionals, researchers, and thought leaders from Malawi and around the globe to explore the forefront of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) and its potential impact on the development of Malawi.

PRESENTATIONS

  1. IntelSurv - An intelligent agent to support disease surveillance · Dr. Amelia Taylor & Chimwemwe Chamangwana
  2. Geospatial methods for Public Health Surveillance in Urban Environments · Patrick Ken Kalonde
  3. A Gentle Introduction to Graph Neural Networks · Colleen M. Farrelly
  4. Pre-Panel Workshop - Security & Privacy Considerations · Dr. Anthony Ziba, Exile Njoka & Eva Mfutso-Bengo
  5. Can ML be used to detect SMS fraud in Malawi? · Amoss Robert
  6. Lifelong and Continual Learning Using Evolving Neuro-Fuzzy Systems · Richard Chilipa
