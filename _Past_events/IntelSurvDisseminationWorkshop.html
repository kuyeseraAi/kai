SUMMARY

This was a research workshop in which we presented the work and findings of the project “AI GRAND CHALLENGES – Practical AI Solutions for Malawi” was held at Amaryllis Hotel in Blantyre on 28th March 2024 from 8:00 am to 4:00 pm. The theme was “Can Generative AI provide solutions for challenges faced in healthcare in Malawi?”. We reported on our work in experimenting with generative AI to provide self-directed access to knowledge for healthcare professionals in Malawi. Among the invitees were our partners at Lilongwe and Blantyre District Health Offices, the Public Health Institute of Malawi, Digital Health, and invitees from academia and industry. The workshop was organised for all those who have been involved in the project.

PRESENTATIONS

  1. IntelSurv Project's Journey From Conception to Reality: Personal Insights · Dr. Thokozani Liwewe
  2. IntelSurv in Action – Demo · Dr. Amelia Taylor & Macphail Magwira
  3. IntelSurv Feedback Sessions · Evie Chapuma & Chisomo Kankhwali
  4. The Integrated Disease Surveillance Approach in Malawi · Mr. Edward Tchado
