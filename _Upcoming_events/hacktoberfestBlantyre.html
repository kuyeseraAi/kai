Join us in Blantyre on 10 October 2026 for Hacktoberfest Blantyre, a day of learning, collaboration and exploration of open-source software and modern programming.

This year’s event brings together developers, students and tech enthusiasts for a hackathon and talks on AI programming, with a focus on open-source models, AI agents, agentic programming and harness engineering.

Whether you're an experienced developer or simply interested in where software development is heading, Hacktoberfest Blantyre is an opportunity to learn new skills, build something meaningful, collaborate with others and explore the future of coding.
