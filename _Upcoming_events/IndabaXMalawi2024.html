This dynamic event is set to take place in the vibrant city of Blantyre, Malawi on August 23 – 24th 2024. The two day gathering hopes to bring together a diverse group of professionals, researchers and thought leaders in Malawi and around the globe to explore the forefront of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) and its potential impact on the development of Malawi.

Since 2019, our mission has been to strengthen and help grow the community in ML and AI in Malawi, to create a space that fosters collaboration, innovation and knowledge exchange.
